# Grand Prix Rules

## Grand Prix Points
### Races
The Grand Prix has 9 races:
- Crazy Lazy
- Kal’s Knoya Ridge Run (The Full Monty is the GP points race; if that is canceled, the longest race held will count. For age categories 17-and-under and 60 and above, the Dome/Original course will also count for G_P_ points)
- Government Peak Climb (ONLY the Up-and-Down event is the GP points race; For age categories 17-and-under and 60 and above, the Uphill Only course will also count for GP points)
- Robert Spurr Memorial Hill Climb (Bird Ridge)
- Juneau Ridge Race
- Mount Marathon Race
- Cirque Series Alyeska
- Matanuska Peak Challenge
- Veins of Gold

### Grand Prix Open Division Points
- Points are awarded for the first 20 men and women in each race based on the FIS Continental Cup scoring system.
- You must finish in the Top 20 of at least 1 race to be eligible for the Grand Prix standings and awards. We count up to 4 races. If you run in more than 4 races, we will count your 4 best performances.
    - 1st place – 100 points
    - 2nd – 90
    - 3rd – 85
    - 4th – 80
    - 5th – 75
    - New Record – 10 points
- For tiebreakers, the top score for an individual race is used, followed by the 2nd best score, if necessary, etc …
- To earn the “Run the Gamut” award, a participant must complete 7 of the 9 Grand Prix events. Completing the Knoya Original Course or Government Peak Uphill Only course will also count for Gamut purposes.
- NOTE: The overall male and female winners of each Grand Prix race earn a bib at the Mount Marathon Race.

### Grand Prix Age Divisions
- Points are awarded to the first 5 individuals in their respective age categories.
- If a birthday divides your age bracket during the GP series, points can be accumulated for 2 age groups.
- You must finish in the Top 5 of at least 1 race to be eligible for the Grand Prix standings and awards. We will count up to 4 races. If you run in more than 4 races, we will count your 4 best performances.
    - 1st – 5 points
    - 2nd – 4
    - 3rd – 3
    - 4th – 2
    - 5th – 1
- For tiebreakers, the top score for an individual race is used, followed by the 2nd best score, if necessary, etc …
- Age groups are
    - 17 and Under
    - 19-29
    - 30-39
    - 40-49
    - 50-59
    - 60-69
    - 70-79
    - 80-89

Typical results file will include the following columns in a CSV, Excel, or PDF format:
- Place
- Bib
- Name
- Age
- Time
- Gender: note, usually gender is not specified on each row, but Male, Female, and Nonbinary results are divided into different sections with a header. The Grand Prix does not currently have a Nonbinary division, but Nonbinary results are supported and will be archived.
